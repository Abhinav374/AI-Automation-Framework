# Worked Example

## Seed

A dictated title: "7 step formula"

Five rough third-person bullets about moving from manual QA into automation.

## Gap to close

The raw input was not fully structured. The skill had to:
- split compound bullets
- normalize to second person
- expand from five bullets to seven steps
- preserve every number mentioned
- convert a buried idea into the core thesis
- build a real receipt from weak input

## What happened

- Expanded 5 bullets to 7 by splitting compound items, including exercises versus projects and GitHub versus LinkedIn.
- Converted third person to second person without inventing new steps.
- Kept all numbers: 90 days, 300 to 400 exercises, 10 per day, 1 hour, 5 projects.
- Extracted the thesis from a closing aside: coding fear dies at repetition volume, not understanding.
- Built a receipt from interviewer feedback: four interviews died at "send us your GitHub".
- Flagged anonymization in the verification block.
- Recommended hook C and used hook B for X placement.
- Reduced the final LinkedIn copy from 292 words to 270 by cutting the low-signal sections.

## Output intent

The point of the example is not that the raw material was polished. The point is that the skill closes the gap between rough input and publish-ready output while remaining faithful to the source material.

## Good takeaway

A strong pack does not need a perfect seed. It needs a clear thesis, a real story, and disciplined editing.
