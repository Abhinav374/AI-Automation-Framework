# Deliverable Specs

## 1. Medium article

Filename: `pack-1-medium-article.md`

Target length: 2,500 to 3,200 words.

Structure:
- Title
- Subtitle in bold style
- Intro: establish the gap
- Story section: real receipt and enough evidence
- Framework or numbered steps
- Caveats
- Bottom line
- CTA in italics

Style requirements:
- strong but readable
- practical and specific
- no em dashes
- no hype filler
- single concrete thesis

## 2. LinkedIn post

Filename: `pack-2-linkedin-post.md`

Target length: 220 to 260 words.

Structure:
- Hook line 1
- Hook line 2
- Optional 4 to 6 item checklist
- The receipt with one undeniable detail
- Short punchline under 8 words
- Counter-argument and pivot
- Definitional reversal
- One-tier offer
- 5 to 7 hashtags

Include first-comment blocks when needed:
- offer asset or link
- offer direct CTA with one link

## 3. LinkedIn image prompts

Filename: `pack-3-linkedin-image-prompts.md`

Create three variants for social cards:
- Variant 1: Hook card, published with the post
- Variant 2: Mechanism card, used in a comment or Instagram style repost
- Variant 3: Closer card, used 5 to 7 days later

Style: style C v2, 4:5 card ratio.

Each card should contain:
- powerful hook headline
- mechanism or proof line
- final closer line

## 4. Medium cover prompt

Filename: `pack-4-medium-image-prompt.md`

Use style A, 16:9 cyber infographic.

Requirements:
- clean cyber aesthetic
- high contrast
- strong title on top
- one supporting stat or sentence
- practical visual hierarchy
- not cluttered

## Final quality checks

Before publishing:
- grep em dash count equals zero
- grep banned phrases equals zero or intentional exceptions
- confirm word counts
- run the Chai test
- run the BFSI CTO test
- run the next sprint test
